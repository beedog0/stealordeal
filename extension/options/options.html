Fairmark settings

Adds up to 90 days of Pokémon price history and graded (PSA, BGS, CGC) prices from pokemonpricetracker.com. The key stays in this browser and is only sent to that site. Each product you look at uses about 2 credits (3 for graded listings), and results are reused for 12 hours.

Buying

Fairmark adds shipping and this tax rate to the listing price to get what you'd really pay. "Pay at most" is the highest price (or bid) that still leaves you this far under market after shipping and tax.

Response details for troubleshooting

The layout of the test response, with no key and no personal data. If history or graded prices don't show up, copy this and send it to whoever maintains Fairmark.
